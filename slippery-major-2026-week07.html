Slippery Nipple League (Major) 2026, Week SEVEN: The Significance of FIRST TIMES

Posted by the Commissioner · Sunday, October 25, 2026

I thought about putting “press 11 in the chat” if you read this – as a test, to see who is reading these and if anyone cares – but I remembered I’m not 25 anymore, and these are just as much for me as they are for y’all.

That said…

We all remember our first time, don’t we? Maybe not every last detail, but you remember the important stuff – the nerves, the build-up, not having the first clue what you were doing but trying to act like you’d been there before, the butterflies and the jitters taking turns – while you watched the clock and considered praying you could just hang on long enough to not be remembered in this regard – and then it was over. You then lay there wondering if it was really as bad for her as the look on her face suggested. The voice in the back of your head then whispered softly to you, “Sorry to break it to you – it was much worse.”

Which brings me to Caiden.

Now, I can’t emphasize this enough – this league itself is actually older than Caiden. Let that sink in for a sec. When the Slippery Nipple League got its start back in 2003, the rest of us were checking box scores in the Times-Picayune on Monday mornings and calling each other on landlines to argue about trades – and Caiden was, well.. not anything yet. Seriously – he’s never known a world without live win probability, and he’s never once had to wait until Tuesday to find out who won.

But in all honesty, I have to give Caiden a ton of credit, because after an (0-2) start – and more than a few late-night texts to the hotline swearing he was done, he was out, he was never touching this thing again – he did what every one of us eventually has to learn to do.

And look, I get it – we’ve ALL been there. Every man in this league has, at one point or another, looked down at the thing in his hand at 1 o’clock in the morning – completely spent, a little ashamed of himself – and sworn up and down that this was the last time, that he was done for good, that he was gonna take up a hobby and maybe go outside for once and be a better man – and that lasts what, a day? A day and a half, tops? Because sure enough, by Tuesday you’re right back at it – just a little peek at first, nothing serious, you’re just “checking on things” – and by Wednesday night you’re elbow-deep in it again like nothing ever happened. I’ve personally sworn off it more times than I can count over the last 23 years, and yet here I am at six in the morning on a Sunday with it in my hand as we speak – because nobody ever really quits, you just take breaks of varying lengths and lie to yourself about it in between.

And that’s exactly what Caiden did – he put it down for a minute, collected himself and came back to it, and this time he slowed down, stopped rushing it, took his time, panicked the perfect amount and took things one step at a time. Next thing you know, he’s coming at us hard: three wins in his last four, which sets the scene for today – and what’s at stake.

For the very first time in his Slippery Major career, Caiden is one good Sunday away from going over (.500) – and I’ll be the first to say it: nobody in this league will be happier for him than the commish if he pulls it off.

For the rest of us, this is our last chance to have a say in how our first half of the season went. Some of us started hot and cooled down – and some of us are just getting started.

Welcome to Week SEVEN. Onto the predictions:

Caiden wins a SUPER CLOSE GAME by less than 2 points, and gets over (.500) for his VERY FIRST TIME in Slippery Major!
