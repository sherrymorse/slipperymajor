Slippery Nipple League (Major) 2026, Week Three: Separation Sunday

Posted by the Commissioner · Sunday, September 27, 2026

There is a point in every fantasy season when the standings stop being a crowded snapshot and start revealing the gap between teams. Some of us are glad to see this, others are not – and others are simply lying to themselves.

For the first few weeks, everyone is still close enough to preserve the illusion. I’m not quite talking about Jesus burned into toast, or anything – but there is a certain beauty to the way things unfold such that we can thank the man above for the gifts he has bestowed upon us.

In the time before now, a bad team can call itself unlucky, a good team can call itself unproven, and the entire league can remain compressed into one tight little mass of possibility.

Then comes Separation Sunday.

This is the first Sunday when the space between teams becomes meaningful enough to change how the standings are read and the relative strength of each team is perceived. The distance starts to matter. What was once merely a cluster begins to take on a more suggestive shape.

Philosophers have always been fascinated by separation because distance reveals things proximity can hide. Once enough space opens between two positions, the eye naturally begins measuring it. Once the arrangement becomes pronounced enough, pretending not to notice it starts requiring more effort than simply acknowledging what is plainly there.

That is where we are now.

By Monday, some teams will have created room for themselves at the top. Others will remain compressed in the middle, still close enough that one good week can change the entire arrangement.

And a few may discover that what looked like a small opening in September has become considerably wider than anticipated. Nothing is settled yet. Positions change. People rise, people fall, and those currently beneath others may eventually find themselves on top.

But this is the first week when the hopium/copium starts to wear off, and what’s left in it’s wake is the need for action – and not excuses, to turn things around.
(or, keep them rolling if you’re Daniel and David)

Welcome to Separation Sunday.

…on to the commish’s weekly predictions:
