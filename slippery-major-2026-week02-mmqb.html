Slippery Nipple League 2026, Week 2 MMQB:Daniel Begins Title Defense with a W, Walter/David/Andrew (The Holy Triumverate) Make BIG Statements

Posted by the Commissioner · Week One in review

1.5 points. That's the differential that separated the three who made the loudest statements in Week One – all without laying a finger on one another. Yep, the three peas in a pod were off in separate continents beating other people by 61, 46, and 56. And of course in any zero sum game, there is always someone on the receiving end of it. This time it was Jason, Caiden and Mark.

Rumor has it Andrew, Walter and David had wrapped up their respective games so easily, they were able to get together at an undisclosed location (pictured below):

In other news, Daniel – who will eventually join the three of them, successfully began his title defense by pulling out a highly competitive and tight match against the always-tough Binh. As I said in the Sunday morning write-up, Daniel's team is sneaky good and there is a good chance he could walk away from this season as the back to back champion technically for what would be the third time, overall (he won three straight a little more than a decade ago – so that counts as two). After a couple of really lean years, things are on the up and up again for the Slippery Major equivalent of Bill Belichick.

The Bowman blast ended in Matt being the victor against Zack. Even with Trey McBride, it's hard to overcome being outscored so badly at the RB position – a problem Zack (and a lot of other people) didn't expect for him to have so soon in the season. Matt's team was always one that we knew would be a very tough out for any of us IF he hit on Justin Jefferson. Well, he hit on Justin Jefferson.

And congrats to Scott for another continuing to find ways to win, no matter what week it is, what league it is, or who his opponent is. I've always said Scott is operating on a higher wavelength than most of us. Year after year, he's always floating around the top contenders. In fact, I can't remember too many seasons over the last 15 years or so that Scott's team wasn't highly competitive – even in his “off” years/seasons. Nice job, again, Scott!

To everyone who struggled last week – we know all too well Week One will be long forgotten when all is said and done. It's ok to lose the battle if you have your eyes on winning the war.

Onward we march to Week Two!

-Commish
