Slippery Nipple League 2026, Week One: Face First into the Mechanism

Posted by the Commissioner · Sunday, September 13, 2026

Sometimes in life, it behooves you to take inventory and look in the mirror at what time exposes in all of us. Some of us are green with envy. Others are red with passion, but today I want to focus on one thing time exposes in all of us: the things we take for granted.

We talk a lot of shit about how ready we are for the season to be over, but without fail 3-6 weeks into the cold turkey coffin we call the offseason, one truth remains: anyone who says thoughts haven't begun creeping in about how much they miss fantasy – is either lying or isn't being honest with themselves.

Let me be clear, though: seven of anything is both a lot to hand over and a lot to take, depending on your perspective. Andrew might disagree, but from my perspective – it's great to be back, and seven months is just way too long to wait.

But that's what being grateful is for – knowing how to savor the moment and appreciate what's in front of us. And right here, right now.. the truth is: we get to do this weekend thirteen more times when all is said and done and we have the whole season in front of us to savor.

John Kramer (Saw IV) once said,

“Cherish your life is the concept that this whole clinic was built on. Cherish your life. Your life.” — John Kramer, Saw IV

And I'm grateful to have built this clinic of misfits we call Slippery Major along with Andrew, for all of us.

Actually, for me the reward is twofold: both in the commissioner's chair writing this nonsense, and in the proverbial saddle as an FF owner, where a beautiful thing happens:

.. every September, the situation we created for ourselves on draft day lives on and reminds us each day that the next five months is our oyster to screw up or ride to glory as we see fit.

Our.. red-rocket to crash or to crash into, if you will. Or, if the national anthems today have you feeling patriotic today… our rockets red glare, so to speak.

Now before I get into the predictions, I know what you're thinking.

No, not that – the other thing:

… what are the odds Jason wrote this himself – and how long will it be before he subcontracts the weekly write up to AI?

… how many weeks of this will Daniel have to sit through before Jason does the whole Jeff bit, again?

Well, if that's the thought you're having I'm sorry to disappoint you but the answer is never.

Not on the Jeff thing – don't worry, that's coming.

It's just that I refuse to use AI to write this – not because I don't want to make my life easier, but because the mechanism actually sucks at recreating something that can't be replicated: me. – and the feeling I get when reading and writing these.

For me, this is just fun.

And no matter who the creator is – Cameron, myself, or anyone else who wants to get face first into the business the way a McNulty would (I've enjoyed every second of Cameron's write up the last few years, btw!) – AI would likely do an equally shitty job at recreating you (yes, you) too because, well – sometimes the mechanism just isn't all it's cracked up to be.

Slippery Major, and the mechanism we created on the other hand?

It's a thing of beauty – it simply reveals what you have cultivated, and what is standing right in front of it.

It leaves no one else to blame but you for the decisions you've made.

And the chat is your mechanism to complain and bitch and moan about what you see fit, and there's nothing China or a flock camera or your nosey neighbor can do about it.

Because – in the end, the mechanism known as Slippery Major reveals all.

Last season it revealed that Daniel was once again serious about kicking ass and taking names in our league, like he used to. (Congrats to Daniel, by the way)

And by all measures, he looks to have done the same thing again here in 2026.

So without further adieu, sit back and warm the seat up on that porcelain mechanism you're on – or perhaps kiss your wife/gf/pet laying next to you, and then turn over for five minutes while I go to town on this crimson rocket of a week one slate the fantasy gods have blessed us with here in Slippery Major.
