Slippery Nipple League (Major) 2026, Week FIVE: Give Me FUEL, Give Me FIRE

Posted by the Commissioner · Sunday, October 11, 2026

Let’s turn the clock back for a sec. Twenty-three years ago, it really didn’t take much.

One game on the tube, the Times-Picayune and the NFL.com box scores on Sunday night, and the commish hunched over a calculator doing every score by hand – then tacking the results packet up in the back at Chili’s, where only half the league could actually see it. The other half just had to take my word for it. (Some of you still do, and I appreciate the trust.) That was enough to carry a man all the way through to the next Sunday. We were young, the stakes were low and, truth be told, the highs were.. modest.

Nobody in this league is wired like that anymore. Not even close.

Somewhere along the way – and I don’t think any of us can say exactly when – the habit got a whole lot bigger than we were. One game became every game. Sunday night’s box scores became a live feed with win probability ticking in real time. And the soundtrack changed too. What used to be the Fox theme is now, in more than one household I could name, something with blast beats and a guitar player who looks vaguely familiar – the kind of guy who wears sunglasses indoors, calls himself an “entrepreneur,” and has strong opinions about your lineup that nobody asked for – shredding away at full volume from the opening kick to the last snap of the late window.

I’ve always said there’s an action junkie in all of us – the commish very much included. And make no mistake, the tolerance only ever moves in one direction. What used to get us through a whole Sunday now barely gets us to halftime.

Philosophers call it hedonic adaptation. Around here, we just call it October.

And so, here we are. Week five has always been the week it really takes hold. The contenders start separating from the pretenders, seven of us are sitting at 2-2, and every single one of those owners has the same look in his eye – wired, a little twitchy, already running the math on what it’s going to take to still be standing come Monday night.

So crank the music, hit refresh and settle in – we’re all in this together. Some of us bet the lines. Some of us blur the lines. And some of us, when Sleeper itself just isn’t enough, do them right off the desk.

Rest assured, all lines will be either considered or consumed. Just like Dusty and his trust Dyson nose would want it.

Welcome to Week Five, Boys!
