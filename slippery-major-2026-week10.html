Slippery Nipple League (Major) 2026, Week TEN: Face First into the Stretch Run

Posted by the Commissioner · Sunday, November 15, 2026

There comes a point in every season – usually right around now, with the leaves turning and the bye weeks thinning out – where a man has to stop talking about what he’s gonna do and actually get down there and do it. Nine weeks of foreplay are behind us now, and while a few of you have been perfectly content to just lay back, close your eyes and let the schedule do all the work, the commish is here to tell you those days are over – because from here on out, nobody’s getting anything handed to them.. you have to be willing to put your face in it.

And let’s be honest with ourselves for a sec – some men are just built for this kind of work, and some men aren’t. Some guys go in with a plan, a little patience and a genuine appreciation for the craft – taking their time, reading the situation, paying attention to the little things that everyone else overlooks, and not coming up for air until the job is done. Others go in like a dog at a water bowl, all tongue and no technique, lapping at the waiver wire every Wednesday at 3 AM and wondering why nobody ever seems satisfied by Monday night. I won’t say which is which – you all know who you are – but I will say that 23 years of this league have taught me one thing above all others: it’s never the guy who talks the most about it that delivers when it counts.

Which brings me to the standings. Two teams sit up top at (7-2), three more are right behind them at (6-3), and the rest of us are huddled together down there in the bottom half – licking our wounds, hungry for a little something, and staring up at a playoff line that’s starting to look less like a cut line and more like a tasting menu. Five weeks left on the schedule – five courses, if you will – and every one of them matters now. No more nibbling. No more sniffing around the edges. If you’re gonna eat, eat.

And look – I know a few of you have been going down all season long, and not in the good way. Scott has gone down seven straight times. Cameron has gone down eight out of nine. And while I admire the commitment – truly, there’s something to be said for a man who keeps showing up with an appetite no matter how many times he leaves the table empty – I’d be lying if I said I didn’t hope a few of you finally get a taste of something this week.

As for the commish, I’ll be dining with Daniel this week in what I can only describe as a meal for two – the main course of Week 10, a (6-3) vs. (7-2) affair with first-round bye implications all over it – and make no mistake about it, I plan on cleaning my plate.

So tuck in your napkins, fellas – it’s about to get messy.

Welcome to Week TEN. Onto the predictions:

Man, I hate to keep featuring my own team in the game of the week, but such is life when you’re always going up against top-ranked or upstart teams, which has been the case for me lately.

Daniel falls into the former, not the latter – and we are no strangers to going up against one another in fantasy football. Early in the 2010s, we played a number of big games against each other. Daniel had just come off either two or three championships in a row, and I was the one who was able to win the crown the following two seasons (Andrew must have been on a break).

Anyway, my point is that while I’ve had some regular-season success the last few years, and while Daniel seems to be back to his prime form once more – we really haven’t had a huge regular-season game like the one we have today in quite some time.

And the best part is that both of us need this game in the worst way. He needs it less than I do, true – but at the end of the day, the winner and loser of this game are going to send shockwaves into the standings in a number of ways.

What’s more, it’s clearly going to be a close one. The one who emerges victorious will most certainly be put to the test in every way imaginable. The only saving grace for whoever has the tough day is that things will be over by the time SNF ends, with no MNF sweat necessary.

And you know what? I’ll take it. I have absolutely no idea who is going to win. All I know is this one might be decided by 2 points or less. And as Daniel said himself, in close games it really does seem like the commish gets the short end of the stick too often – so yes, we will go with that: Daniel wins this all-time clash, by less than one point.

Good luck this week, guys!
