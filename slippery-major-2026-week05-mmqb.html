Slippery Nipple League 2026, Week 5 MMQB:The FANTASTIC FOUR Have ArrivedDaniel Stays Perfect, Walter Off the Schneid, Commish Makes It Three Straight

Posted by the Commissioner · Week Four in review

Every great institution has its traditions. The Masters has the green jacket. The Kentucky Derby has the roses. The Stanley Cup gets passed hand to hand, kissed, cradled, held over the head and paraded in front of the people.

Slippery Major has something a little more.. hands-on.

Every year, at roughly this point in the season, the league pauses to honor the four men who have done the hardest thing in fantasy football: protect what’s theirs. Because anybody can get a lead. Holding onto it is a different animal altogether. It takes a firm grip. One hand – two, in the years Andrew makes it. It takes the courage to stand there completely exposed in front of everybody you know – with nothing but your own palm between you and humiliation – and not flinch.

Philosophers have long debated what a man’s most valuable possessions really are. Some say his name. Some say his word. In fantasy football the answer has always been simpler: they’re the two or three guys you’d never in a million years trade. Your crown jewels. And this year, with half the league’s studs going down before Halloween, the four men at the top are the ones who planned ahead. They knew the day would come when they’d need handcuffs.. and they knew exactly where to put them.

I’ve said it before: four walls build a home. Four brothers make up a formula. And four men, oiled up by early-season success, standing shoulder to shoulder and holding on for dear life – well, that’s a group photo nobody asked for, and couldn’t forget if we tried.

Other leagues make their best wait until December for a trophy. So do we – but we’re not monsters. We give them something to hold onto in the meantime. For four weeks we savor the ominous air that builds before we finally reveal the four men currently on top – and, as is tradition, nobody asks how or why these exact four men are the ones chosen to represent us. That’s because it’s obvious, isn’t it? Just look at the way they accurately depict a random sampling of any four of us, on average.

It’s not glamorous. It’s not dignified. Year after year, those who make it in swear it’s the proudest moment of their fantasy lives.. and those who don’t spend the rest of the season pretending they never wanted to be in it anyway. But make no mistake – every man in this league, at some point, has looked at that GIF and quietly wondered what it would feel like to stand in that lineup.. and, a little more quietly, which of the other three he’d want standing next to him.

This year, four of you no longer have to imagine it. No more practicing the pose alone in the bathroom mirror with the door locked – this time, you get to do it in front of everyone you love.

Grip it. Cup it. Shield it like a match in an October hurricane. Some of you will need both hands for this. Most of you, if we’re being honest, won’t. Positions change on a dime around here, and nothing in the Slippery is guaranteed to stay in your hands for long.

LADIES AND GENTLEMAN I PRESENT TO YOU...

Your 2026 Slippery Nipple (Major) FANTASTIC FOUR:

Congrats to all our winner this week, including Walter for getting off the schneid in dominant fashion to, at least for the moment, return the air of invincibility he carried with him the first couple of weeks. Binh’s team has been on a bit of a rollercoaster ride so far this season, but the cart was pointing UP this week – can he keep it going next week against Cameron, who has to be one of the toughest (0-4) opponents ever assembled, in our league. Also, shout out to Caiden for getting his second win in a row, and saving his season entirely after the (0-2) start. It’s not easy to get out of a hole in this league, so kudos to him for scoring just enough to squeak by no-longer-that-young Zack. Someone who did the opposite of squeak by, for the zillionth time this season, was Daniel, whose dominant team is sort of not fair as a follow up to last seasons championship team. His early success does, however, contrast with the lack thereof in the early going last season in Daniel’s championship run. From midseason last year until now, Daniel has been nearly unbeatable.

It was good to see Matt’s team put in a good showing after it looked like they were going to trail off a cliff the last couple of weeks leading into this. If he can somehow master the artform of fantasy consistency this season – his team is always one to watch down the stretch. He’s had some epic 2nd half runs, so that’s something we should all be aware of. And last but not least congrats to MOI for snagging my third win in a row. Mark was a tough opponent and I knew it wouldn’t be easy at any point during the sweat. It took until Monday night to settle this one, as expected, but Bijan Robinson is simply on another level – and showed his value and why I made him the #2 overall pick.

I might make another post before Sunday, but until then enjoy the fantastic imagery and GOOD LUCK TO EVERYONE in WEEK 5.

-Commish
