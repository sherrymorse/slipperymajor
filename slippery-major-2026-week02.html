Slippery Nipple League 2026, Week Two: Waist First into the Grind

Posted by the Commissioner · Sunday, September 20, 2026

If week one was about savoring things and looking ahead at what's to come, week two is a two-fold mix of savoring last week's win and finding a way to build on it – or, like me, finding something to cling to and build on from dumpster fire that was week one.

At the end of the day, we all have a choice. We can look outward to see if there's something to be learned, or someone we can blame – or, my preference, we can look deep within ourselves and cling to something else and embracing the grind that life is, and refuse to do anything else but enjoy what's in front of us – the wet and bumpy road that it is.

So here is what I propose we do about it. Find someone you love, or someone you trust. Hand them a pair of scissors. Then, I want you to find a second pair, and grab them for yourself. Then I want you to either print out week one's scores or write “Week One” onto a piece of paper and crumple it up into a little ball.

Then, you and your loved/trusted one will spread the legs of the scissors and simultaneously plunge them forward into the rolled up knot of timber, opening and closing the scissors in a grinding fashion, ensuring that nothing remains when it is all said and done – except you and your loved/trusted one with a pair of scissors in your hand…

… and all of the time you both have lost track of when you wake up and realize that that the piece of paper you were both once shredding is long gone, and you two could have stopped ages ago, but you didn't.

All that remains, then, is week two.

Onward we go, then Scissoring into the Second Sunday of the Season.. and the predictions sitting on the commissioner's desk: (4-2, .666 so far in 2026)
