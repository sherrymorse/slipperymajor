Slippery Nipple League (Major), Week 4: Wide Open in Week 4 for the Scoop You’ve Been Waiting For

Posted by the Commissioner · Sunday, October 4, 2026

The dining room has always been sacred ground. It's where families gather, where bread is broken, where an attractive older woman who wore thigh high stockings and worked at Dillards used to sit, have coffee and purposely cross her legs in a very visceral manner — admittedly, a lot for 17 year old me to take. And so, in this very dining room I learned to split the difference in various other ways and with other people.

The dining room, where grace is said.. and where, every so often, two grown men arrive at an understanding that no amount of grace could ever undo. So many memories, so little videotape to refer back as proverbial bookmarks for it all.

Oh, if those walls could talk……

Philosophers have argued for centuries about what truly separates a man from his dignity. Some say pride. Some say money. I'm here to tell you it's roughly eighteen inches of air between his feet and the floor – and a friend willing to hold him there.

That brings us to the most important concept in fantasy football: the pickup, and the scoop. One must always have their ear to the ground in the game of life and the game of fantasy football.

Every week, somebody out there is lifting something off the ground that nobody else wanted to touch. Scooping him up, you might say. He gets low, slides both arms underneath, commits fully – and once it's up, there's no graceful way to put it down. You just hold it there and hope nobody walks in.

The great ones always see it before anyone else. Some of us were known for always getting the scoop first, back in the day. Funny how life works. Sometimes you get the scoop.. and sometimes, the scoop gets you.

Still, I'm not bitter. I've done my best thinking with both feet off the ground. There's a certain clarity that only comes when your legs are being handled by someone else. You stop worrying about the projections. You stop checking the injury report. You simply surrender to the moment, stare at the ceiling fan and think about what you'll have for dessert.

Whoever wins today will be FEELING FANTASTIC. The other one will be picked up, looked over – and quietly put back down.

Welcome to Week Four. Onto the predictions:
