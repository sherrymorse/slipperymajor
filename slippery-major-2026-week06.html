Slippery Nipple League (Major) 2026, Week SIX: Bye/Bi Weeks are in FULL SWING

Posted by the Commissioner · Sunday, October 18, 2026

Through six weeks, for the most part everything is in order. If you have a winning record, you would be in the playoffs – if they started today. And yes, there are a couple of teams at the top and bottom who absolutely could end up flipping by season’s end – but what the great power that is TIME is for. Each week that rolls by gets us closer to a truth that reconciles with the strength of the team being measured across it.

Teams with weak drafts but strong in-season management begin to rise. Teams with strong drafts with below average in-season management become exposed by the ticking clock, and each move we make. That’s the beauty of fantasy football and the sheer length of the season, and what it brings out in us – and the champions we breed here, in the Slippery Nipple.

And of course, bye weeks sucks – but it’s a necessary evil in the midst of a long season. Having said that, not all bye weeks are created equal. Weeks 11, 6, 13 and 7 are by far the worst. These are the weeks which have any combination of the most teams on a bye, and the most fantasy points being subtracted from starting rosters that given week. So, while Week 11 is the one who will see the most teams on a bye, Week 6 is definitely up there with teams who per capita pack the most devastating punch when it’s players subtracted from fantasy lineups.

This week, various teams will be without, in no particular order: Jamar Chase, Jamyr Gibbs, Tee Higgins, Joe Burrow, Chase Brown, Amon Ra St. Brown, Jared Goff, Sam Laporta, Justin Jefferson, Jameson Williams, just to name a FEW. There are more.

That’s a lot of firepower subtracted from our respective arsenals and when it’s all said and done, there’s no question this will impact the scoreboard across the league in a negative way.

Having said that, having a deep bench is part of what makes a fantasy champion, so those who had deep drafts and good waiver picks ups may not even miss a beat this week. Some will put themselves in a position to still have a fighting chance, in spite of losing their STARS. And, some unfortunately will realize just how limp-dick their bench is, unlike the people shown above.

We keep all of these things in mind, and we adapt and adjust as we head into the HALLOWEEN DARKNESS that lurks around the corner, ahead – and, commish’s WEEK 6 PREDICTIONS:
